How to Make Cookies

Jump to ingredients · Steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix the flour and sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add the butter, egg, and vanilla extract. Mix until smooth.
  4. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet.
  5. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until golden brown.

Why This Recipe

I enjoy baking cookies with my friends and family. A specific memory I have is from when I did a Blind Deaf and Mute Baking challenge with my family friend and younger brother.
