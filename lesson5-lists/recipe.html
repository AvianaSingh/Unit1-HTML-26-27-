How to Make Cookies

I picked cookies because I enjoy eating them. They taste really good.

  • Go to the Ingredients
  • Go to the Steps
  • Go to the Notes

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Salt
  • Baking Soda
  • Optional: Chocolate Chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degree Fahrenheit
  2. Mix the wet ingredients until they are light and fluffy
  3. Combine the dry ingredients in a separate bowl.
  4. Blend the two mixtures together by slowly adding the dry mix into the wet mixture until a dough forms. Fold in the chocolate chips (optional)
  5. Scoop the dough into small balls and place them 2-3 inches apart on a prepared baking sheet
  6. Bake in the oven for 10-12 minutes until the edges are golden brown
  7. Cool the cookies on the pan for 3 minutes, then move them to a wire track
  8. Enjoy your cookies!

Notes

  • Equipment needed
    • Measuring Cups and Spoons
    • Digital Kitchen Scale
    • Mixing Bowls
    • Spatule or Wooden
    • Baking Sheets
    • Cookie Scoop
    • Oven
    • Wire Cooling Rack
  • Serving suggestions
    • Eat cookies with cold milk
    • Eat cookies with hot tea or coffee
    • Eat cookies with strawberries
    • Eat cookies with vanilla or mint ice cream in between a pair of cookies.
    • Eat cookies with a side of salted caramel, warm fudge, or raspberry coulis
